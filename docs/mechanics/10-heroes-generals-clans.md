## The hero

Every player has **one hero**, chosen before the run from a roster of ten.
Within a group, each hero can be taken by only one player.

- **Leading.** The hero leads the garrison of the village where it stands, or
  the army it marches with. Its effects, skills and gear add to those troops'
  bonuses for battles there, and to scouts and caravans sent from there.
- **Fighting.** It also fights in the ranks as a **champion**: 150 attack,
  80 defense and 1,600 health at level 1, +10% a level
  ([page 6](06-battles.md#champions-heroes-and-generals-in-the-ranks)).
- **Travelling.** On its own it moves at 6 hexes an hour.

- **XP** comes from enemies killed in the battles the hero fights in.

### Wounds

- **The hero never dies.** When its side loses, or its champion is struck down,
  it is **wounded** for 8 game hours and carried home.
- **Shorter wounds.** Volkh −50%, Ilya −30%, and 8% less per Hero's Hall level
  in the village where it recovers.

### Quests

The hero offers **3 quests** at a time, new ones every 8 game hours.

- **How a quest runs.** The hero takes the road there and back, spends the
  quest's hours on the spot, and comes home with XP. With the quest's **risk**,
  it comes home wounded instead.
- **Resource rewards** grow +10% for every hero level above 1.

| Quest | Away | Distance | XP | Risk | Brings |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ambush the horde's road | 4 h | 5 | 40 | 25% | The front's next horde sets out 6 game hours later, and hates more for it |
| Ride beyond the border | 3 h | 6 | 25 | 15% | A full report on the front's next horde |
| Find the lost caravan | 3 h | 4 | 20 | 10% | 300 / 300 / 300 / 150 |
| The smith's hoard | 5 h | 4 | 30 | 20% | A piece of gear the hero lacks |
| Search the ruins | 4 h | to the site | 35 | 15% | An unguarded ancient site searched |
| Hunt the great boar | 4 h | 3 | 30 | 20% | 700 grain |
| Collect the hill clans' tribute | 5 h | 6 | 25 | 10% | 100 wood, 400 stone, 300 iron |
| Pilgrimage to Perun's oak | 8 h | 5 | 80 | 5% | XP only |

- **The ambush quest** grows riskier and richer with the front's hatred:
  +60% × hatred to the risk (at most 70%), and XP ×(1 + 3 × hatred).
- **Calling a quest off.** It can be called off before the hero turns for
  home. The hero comes back with nothing, no XP and no wound.

### Army quests

While the hero leads an army standing in the field, it offers **army
quests** instead: 2 at a time, one at a time.

- **How they run.** Taking one raises a place 3–9 hexes away, held by enemies
  that grow with the hero's level, and the army marches on it.
- **Deadline.** Done in time, it gives XP and resources (or a hard ruin's
  finds). Not done in time, the place is gone.

| Quest | Distance | Defenders | Time | XP | Brings |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Break the raiders' warband | 3–6 | 25 + 4/level | 24 h | 70 | 600 / 600 / 600 / 300 |
| Hunt down the Nav warlord | 5–9 | 45 + 6/level | 36 h | 140 | 1,200 / 1,200 / 1,000 / 800 |
| Storm the haunted barrow | 4–8 | 35 + 5/level | 36 h | 110 | A hard ruin's finds |

## Generals

Generals (voivodes) are lesser heroes recruited at a **Hero's Hall**.

- **How many.** A player keeps one general per Hero's Hall, plus one more for
  every 2 levels of it.
- **Recruiting** takes 2 game hours. It costs 250 / 200 / 250 / 100, ×1.5 for
  every general the player already has. A recruit can be given a name.
- **Levels, no skills.** Up to level 10, from kills: level L needs
  40 × 1.4^(L−1) XP. Each level gives the troops it commands **+2% attack and
  defense**. Generals have no skills, gear or quests.
- **Commanding.** A general leads the army it marches with, or commands the
  garrison of the village it stands in (the highest-level general there).
  Its effects count only when no hero leads those troops.
- **In the ranks.** It fights as a champion: 90 attack, 55 defense and
  1,000 health, +8% a level.
- **Generals die.** A general whose side loses a battle, or whose champion is
  struck down, is killed. When the hero falls, the general with it takes
  command.
- **Moving.** Generals travel between your villages at 6 hexes an hour.
