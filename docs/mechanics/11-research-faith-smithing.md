# 11. Research, faith and the Blacksmith

[← Heroes, generals and clans](10-heroes-generals-clans.md) · [Index](README.md) · Next: [Scouting, exploration and expansion →](12-scouting-and-expansion.md)

## Research

Research is **shared by the group**: one tech at a time, and every finished
tech helps every member's villages.

### Research points

- **Academies.** Each member's Academy brings in research points: 30 per hour
  at level 1, ×1.35 a level, up to level 5.
- **The pool.** The members' points make up the group's pool.

### Starting and paying for a tech

1. **Starting it.** A member starts a tech from a village with an Academy of
   the tech's level, once the group knows every tech it requires and, for a
   rare tech, has found its lore scroll.
   - **Research cost.** 0.1 research point for every game second the tech
     lasts, taken from the pool.
   - **Embers.** The mightiest techs also take Svarog's Embers, paid by the
     member who starts them.
2. **Paying its resources.** Any member sends what they can of the tech's
   resource cost, from any village.
3. **The study.** Once it is all paid, the study begins. It lasts the tech's
   game seconds, shortened by `researchTime` bonuses (Volkh, Gavran) or
   lengthened by maluses (Rys, Marya).

## Lore scrolls

**Lore scrolls** are found at ancient sites and in raids on the North
([page 12](12-scouting-and-expansion.md#ancient-sites)).

- **Once per world.** Each is found once, and serves the whole group.
- **Effects** add to every member's bonuses.

| Scroll | Effect |
|---|---|
| Song of the Sowers | +5% grain |
| Ledger of Veles | Caravans carry 15% more |
| Oath of Perun | +5% attack |
| Weave of Mokosh | +10% storage |
| Hymn of Dazhbog | +5% defense |
| Winds of Stribog | Unlocks the rare tech Wind Riders |
| Rite of Svarog's Forge | Unlocks the rare tech Ember-Forged Arms |

## Svarog's Embers

The rare resource. **Each player keeps their own**: whoever's army finds one
keeps it.

| Found in | Chance |
|---|---|
| A cleared lair | 75% |
| A searched ancient site | 15–30% depending on the site, divided by the number of players |
| A battle won raiding the North | 8% (the stronghold ×3) |

Spent on:
- **The bridge:** its later stages ([page 14](14-bridge-and-endgame.md)).
- **The ritual torc:** the last stage's quest.
- **Ember techs:** the mightiest techs above.
- **The Shrine:** its levels and changing its god.

## The Shrine

A shrine to one of the old gods.

- **Building it.** It needs Elder's Hall 3 and goes up to level 5. Every level
  takes **one Ember** (returned in full if cancelled), and mostly grain, the
  offering.
- **Choosing a god.** Once it stands, its village picks one god. The god's
  effects × the Shrine's level add to the **player's** bonuses in all their
  villages. Turning to another god takes an Ember. Tearing the Shrine down
  ends the blessing.

| God | Per Shrine level |
|---|---|
| Perun, the thunderer | +2% attack |
| Svetovid, the four-faced watcher | +3% defense at home, +1% defense |
| Veles, lord of herds | +3% grain, troops eat 2% less |
| Mokosh, who spins fate | +5% storage, raiders take 4% less |
| Svarog, the heavenly smith | Build and train 3% faster |
| Dazhbog, the giving sun | +2% of everything |
| Stribog, grandfather of the winds | Caravans and scouts +5% speed, scouts +2 points survival |

## The Blacksmith

Each player forges **improvements for their own units**, one at a time, from
a village with a Blacksmith. What is forged serves all that player's villages
and armies.

### Stat lines

- **Which lines.** Every trainable unit has a line for each stat it has:
  attack, defense and health (where its own value is above 0), and speed
  (every unit that moves).

| Line | Name | Per level |
|---|---|---|
| Attack | Sharper arms | +10% attack |
| Defense | Stouter armor | +10% defense |
| Health | Hardier troops | +10% health |
| Speed | Better harness | +10% speed |

- **Levels.** Each line has **3 levels**:

| Level | Needs a Blacksmith of level | Cost | Time (game hours) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 | the unit's training cost × 8 | (1 + 0.5 × the unit's tier level) |
| 2 | 5 | × 16 | × 2 |
| 3 | 10 | × 24 | × 3 |

- **Tier level.** The time uses the unit's `requiresLevel`: a Spearman (1)
  takes 1.5 game hours for level 1, a Warlord's Guard (9) takes 5.5.
- **Faster forging.** Each Blacksmith level forges 3% faster.
- **Example.** Spearman attack level 1 costs 280 wood, 160 stone, 240 grain
  and 240 iron.
- **Adding up.** Forged attack and defense add to research bonuses: a unit
  with +10% from research and +20% forged fights at +30%.
- **Speed.** Forged speed sets the pace of armies, scouts and settlers.

### Fire arrows and poisoned blades

Two more lines add an **affliction** to a unit's blows
([page 6](06-battles.md#burn-and-poison)):

| Line | For | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Fire arrows | Archers and siege engines | Its blows burn: 10% of each hit again in each of the next 3 rounds |
| Poisoned blades | Infantry and cavalry | Its blows poison: the side it strikes fights 15% weaker for 2 rounds |

- **One level each.** It needs a Blacksmith of **level 10**, and is priced and
  timed like a stat line's level 3.
- **Already poisonous.** Units that poison already (Poison Archer, Poison
  Hunter) have no poison line.

### One at a time

- **One job per player.** A player's Blacksmiths forge one improvement at a
  time, whichever village it was started from.
- **When it finishes,** the player is told, and the bonus applies at once
  everywhere.
