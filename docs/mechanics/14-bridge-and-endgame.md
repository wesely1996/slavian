# 14. The Kalinov Bridge and the endgame

[← Events and raids beyond the edge](13-events-and-raids.md) · [Index](README.md) · Next: [Player against player →](15-player-vs-player.md)

## The story

Beyond the Holy City the Kalinov Bridge crosses the Smorodina, the river of
fire, from Nav to Yav, the living world, and every horde walks over it. The
volkhvs mean to raise it on from Yav to **Prav**, the realm of the gods. Once
the Ritual Arch joins it to Prav, nothing from Nav can set foot on it again.
The hordes know this, and will throw everything at the city on the night the
arch is bound.

## How a stage is raised

The bridge rises in **five stages**, one at a time. A stage opens when the
one before it stands. To raise it, while it is open, the group must:

1. **Pay its cost** in resources, drawn from the **Holy City vault**. Caravans
   bring resources there ([page 4](04-economy-and-logistics.md#the-holy-city-vault)).
2. **Give its Embers:** Svarog's Embers donated by members.
3. **Do its quest.**

Then the stage takes **12 game hours** to raise.

### The quests

- **The Builders' Rune (relic)**
  - **What.** Explore the **Builders' Barrow**, a kurgan raised when the
    stage opens, 5–8 hexes from the Holy City.
  - **Defended by** 30 of the North's dead.
  - **Done** when an army searches it while the stage is open.
- **The Den under the Sacred Oaks (grove)**
  - **What.** Clear the quest lair, 5–8 hexes out.
  - **Defended by** 36 of the North's beasts.
- **Beat the Smith-Breakers (defense)**
  - **The host.** A host of 40, apart from the hordes, sets out 3 game hours
    after the stage opens and marches on the Holy City.
  - **Done** when it is beaten.
  - **Not beaten?** Another comes 12 game hours later.
- **The Eye of Veles (artifact)**
  - **What.** Raid the North to its full depth (3 fields) and sack its
    stronghold, while the stage is open ([page 13](13-events-and-raids.md#raids-beyond-the-edge)).
- **Craft the ritual torc (ritual)**
  - **What.** Forge the torc from 1 Svarog's Ember.

### While a stage is raised

- **Construction raids.** During its 12 hours, **every active front sends one
  small horde** at a different target: 5 + 2 × the stage's number units. The
  group's villages come first, then the Holy City.
- **Bigger waves.** Every finished stage makes waves **10% bigger**
  ([page 9](09-hordes-and-threats.md#wave-size)).

## Ritual Night

When the Ritual Arch stands, **Ritual Night** begins:

- **Every front at once.** The waves still scheduled are cancelled. 6 game
  hours later (after quiet hours), every active front sends one horde
  **1.5×** the usual size, all setting out together. Each picks its target as
  any horde does.
- **Holding.** The rite is checked every game hour until the night is over.
- **The win.** If the Holy City still stands (Sanctity above 0) when the last
  horde is beaten, **the group wins**:

> *The last horde broke against the walls and the rite was finished. The
> Kalinov Bridge runs from Nav through Yav to Prav, the gods' light holds it
> end to end, the road from Nav is shut, and the bells of the Holy City ring
> for the first time in a generation.*

## Losing

The run is lost the moment the Holy City's **Sanctity reaches 0**. Every
horde that wins there takes Sanctity for each of its surviving soldiers, more
the higher their tier ([page 1](01-world-and-run.md#sanctity-damage)). The
Avatar of Černobog, if it gets there, destroys the Holy City outright.

> *The walls are breached and the sanctuary fires are out. The hordes pour
> over the Kalinov Bridge unopposed. Some of the people escaped into the
> forests, carrying the songs with them; perhaps one day they will try again.*

Losing villages never ends a run: a player with only ruins gets a refugee
camp ([page 3](03-villages-and-buildings.md#ruins-and-rebuilding)).
