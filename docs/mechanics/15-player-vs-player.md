# 15. Player against player

[← The Kalinov Bridge and the endgame](14-bridge-and-endgame.md) · [Index](README.md)

A world created with **PvP** on lets players attack each other's villages.
In a co-op world (the default) nobody can. Solo runs are always co-op.

## Protection

- **A new village is safe** from other players for **72 game hours** after
  it is founded: a starting village from the start of the run, a settled one
  from the day its settlers arrive (÷ world speed, like every game hour).
- **What it shows.** Picking a protected village as a target says how long
  it stays safe. An army that arrives while it still is turns home.

## Attacking

Send an army at another player's village with the **Attack** orders. Tick
**Raze it if they win** to burn it, otherwise the army plunders it.

| Orders | If the attackers win |
|---|---|
| Attack | They carry off **30%** of every store, as much as their units can carry (each unit's carry, [page 13](13-events-and-raids.md#raids-beyond-the-edge)) |
| Raze | The village burns to a ruin ([page 9](09-hordes-and-threats.md#raid-or-raze)); a refugee camp is only plundered |

Either way the army then marches home with what it carries.

## The fight

The fight is resolved **at once** when the army arrives, in rounds like any
battle ([page 6](06-battles.md)):

- **The attackers:** the army in its stance, its hero and general fighting
  in the ranks, with its owner's bonuses.
- **The defenders:** the village's garrison in the village's stance (with
  the hero and general standing there) and every army standing in the
  village, each in its own stance and breaking on its own, all with the
  home bonus.
- **The walls.** While the village has walls, the defenders fight behind
  them, with the walls' defense, attack and archer bonuses
  ([page 7](07-walls-and-sieges.md)). Each **battering ram** in the
  attacking army takes a quarter of that off; four rams leave none.
- **The end.** It goes on until one side breaks or the last round passes;
  then the defenders hold. Beaten defenders' armies fall back home; beaten
  attackers come home with what is left of them.
- **No help in time.** Armies nearby don't answer a player's attack: it is
  over before they could march.

Both players get a push with the outcome, and the battle report lists every
round.

## Rivals, not allies

In a PvP world every other player is a rival:

- **Armies help only their own player's villages.** Supporting,
  reinforcing or harassing at another player's village is refused, and
  armies don't answer battles there.
- **Caravans still go anywhere,** to trade or to pay a rival off.
- **Shared things stay shared:** the Holy City and its vault, research and
  the Kalinov Bridge. The run is still won or lost together.
