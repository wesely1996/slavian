# Privacy policy

Slavian keeps as little about you as it can.

## What is kept

- The display name you choose (or the guest name given to you).
- Sign-in sessions (a hashed token; the plain token lives only on your device).
- The state of the worlds you play in (villages, armies, resources, buildings).
- Your notification settings: quiet hours, digest hour and language.
- A device push token, if you turn on notifications.

## What is not

- No advertising, no tracking pixels, no profiling.
- Nothing is sold or shared with third parties.
- No location, contacts, camera, microphone or other device data.
- Light, anonymous run telemetry only (run start/end, outcome, wave results).

## Your rights

- **Export:** download everything kept about you from the app (**Support and
  privacy → Export my data**). It is saved as a JSON file on your device.
- **Delete:** delete your account and its data from the app (**Support and
  privacy → Delete my account**). The account, its sessions, memberships and
  device tokens go with it. Bug reports you sent are kept without your account
  link so the developer can still fix what you reported.
- **Contact:** send a bug report from the app with the current game state.

## Data retention

Deleted accounts are removed immediately from the live database. Off-site daily
backups roll off within 30 days.

## Children

Slavian is not directed at children under 13. Do not make an account if you are
younger.

## Changes

This policy may change with a new release; the current version ships with the
game.
